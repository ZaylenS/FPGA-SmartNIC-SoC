  Reusable style anchor (paste at top of every prompt)

  STYLE: Warm 2D-animated storybook illustration, Pixar-meets-Studio-Ghibli,
  soft golden-hour lighting, gentle pastel palette (cream, dusty rose, sage green,
  warm amber). Hand-painted texture, slight film grain. Friendly, cozy, slow
  breathing pace. Aspect ratio 16:9. 8 seconds.

  RECURRING CHARACTER: A small fluffy golden retriever with kind brown eyes,
  soft cream-orange fur, a faded blue collar with a tiny silver tag.
  He is calm, alert, gentle. Appears in every shot.

  NEGATIVE: photorealistic human faces, uncanny valley, dark/horror tones,
  glitchy artifacts, neon cyberpunk, text overlays, watermarks, fast cuts,
  frantic camera shake.

  ---
  Clip 1 — Hook (0–8s): cozy kitchen, the call comes in

  A sunlit cozy kitchen at golden hour. A pair of weathered, gentle hands
  (palms only, no face) cradle a steaming ceramic mug at a wooden table.
  A vintage cream-colored landline phone sits beside a vase of daisies.
  The phone begins to ring softly — gentle ringer animation, slight wobble.
  The golden retriever lifts his head from a sunlit floor cushion, ears perking.
  Camera: slow dolly-in from mid-shot to the phone. Quiet, anticipatory mood.

  Clip 2 — Hook continued (8–15s): "Unknown Number"

  Close-up over the shoulder of the smartphone screen lying on the table.
  The screen glows softly: "UNKNOWN NUMBER" in a clean storybook font.
  The golden retriever pads up to the table, places his chin near the phone,
  and tilts his head — one ear up, one ear down, a tiny worried whimper visible
  in his expression. Soft lens flare. Camera: gentle push-in on the dog's eyes.

  Clip 3 — Problem (15–23s): unseen threats float toward the home

  Wide cutaway: a cozy storybook cottage at twilight, warm windows glowing.
  From the dark sky, a small flock of stylized envelopes with shadowy
  cartoon eyes drift menacingly toward the house — slow, floating, not scary,
  just *off*. One envelope has a tiny dollar sign, one has a camera lens,
  one has a stolen photograph peeking out the seal.
  Camera: slow truck-right tracking the envelopes. Mood: gentle unease.

  Clip 4 — Problem close-up (23–30s): what they want

  Inside the cottage living room. A smart speaker on a shelf, a TV with a
  family photo as wallpaper, the smartphone on a side table — all glowing
  warm. The shadowy envelopes from outside press faintly against the window
  glass, peering in. The golden retriever stands between the window and
  the devices, low growl, fur slightly raised. Protective, not aggressive.
  Camera: static, slight zoom-in on the dog's stance.

  Clip 5 — Solution analogy 1 (30–38s): the tiny guard inside

  Macro shot diving INTO the smartphone screen — a magical zoom through
  the glass into a tiny cartoon world inside. There stands a friendly
  chibi-style security guard in a soft blue uniform with a round badge
  shaped like a paw print. The golden retriever sits beside him, tail wagging.
  They stand at a glowing doorway where envelopes line up to enter.
  Camera: dolly-in through the phone screen, magical particle transition.

  Clip 6 — Solution analogy 2 (38–45s): guard sorts good vs. bad

  The tiny guard at the doorway, dog at his side. Good envelopes (one with a
  heart, one with a child's drawing labeled "Grandma!", one with a stethoscope
  icon) walk through cheerfully and a green checkmark appears.
  A shadowy envelope with sneaky eyes tries to sneak through — the guard
  firmly raises his palm, the dog gives one calm bark, and the envelope
  poofs into harmless dust. Camera: medium two-shot, warm key light.

  Clip 7 — Why it matters (45–55s): the chip, the smile, the call

  Pull back from the tiny world: zoom out through the phone screen to reveal
  a glowing pastel-blue microchip nestled inside the phone, pulsing gently
  like a heartbeat. Cut to the same hands from Clip 1 picking up the phone —
  now the screen shows a video call from a grinning cartoon grandchild
  waving "Hi Grandma!" The golden retriever's tail thumps the floor.
  Camera: smooth pull-out then match-cut to the answered call.

  Clip 8 — Scale-up part 1 (55–65s): same chip, bigger houses

  Wide aerial-style storybook map view. The same glowing pastel-blue chip
  icon appears like a friendly stamp on three buildings in sequence:
  a small storybook hospital with a red cross, a classic bank with columns,
  and a vast cartoon data center with rows of softly humming server towers.
  The golden retriever trots along the map between them, leaving tiny
  paw-print sparkles. Camera: slow pan across the landscape.

  Clip 9 — Scale-up part 2 (65–75s): protecting the AI brain

  Inside the cartoon data center: glowing pastel server racks shaped like
  friendly bookshelves, each holding a softly pulsing "AI brain" orb.
  The same shadowy envelopes from earlier try to sneak in carrying tiny
  wrenches (tampering). The tiny guard and the golden retriever stand at
  the entrance, calmly turning them away. One envelope is gently escorted
  out by the dog. Camera: slow tracking shot down the server aisle.

  Clip 10 — Close (75–88s): the watchful friend at sunset

  Return to the cozy kitchen from Clip 1, now at sunset. The golden
  retriever curls up beside the smartphone on the table, eyes half-closed
  but ears still alert. The vintage landline is quiet. Daisies sway slightly
  in the breeze from an open window. A soft glow pulses once from the phone
  — the chip inside, still watching. Camera: very slow pull-back to a wide
  warm composition. Hold on the final frame for an end-card overlay.

  Clip 11 — End card (88–90s): the title

  Static end card, hand-painted storybook style. Centered text in a warm
  serif font: "Security that fits in your pocket." Smaller text below:
  "Group 12 — Zilin Shao." The golden retriever sits in the lower-right
  corner, head tilted, gives one small wag. Soft cream background with
  faint paw-print watermark. Hold 2 seconds.
